Import product prices

Import a CSV with columns item,store,price,reference_price,size,url. Item names must match your pantry staples (case-insensitive). Use only Coles, Woolworths or Costco. Costco rows should use a comparable per-pack equivalent (large multipacks must be normalised). One row per offer. An optional date column records when the price was collected. Import replaces the previous feed but keeps pantry settings. All pricing is treated as user-supplied.

Automated feed

No synced prices yet. Coles and Woolworths prices sync from Majordomo's weekly job; Costco stays manual - update it in chat when prices change, since there is no reliable automated Costco feed.

How recommendations work

For each item, the app finds the lowest current offer, checks it is a genuine special or at its 30-day low (never a questionable one), under your personal price ceiling if you've set one, and cheaper than Costco per pack if a Costco price is on file, then recommends buying enough to reach your target months of stock. Items without a qualifying offer are never recommended. Basket quantities can be adjusted independently. Nothing is ordered automatically.
